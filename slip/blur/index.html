OTO Slip Blur

Mark the spots once per app. After that, every screenshot from that app comes out blurred and ready to post. Images stay on this device and never upload.

App Keep mode

Keep only boxes: everything is blurred except what you box (game, side, odds). Safest, since a new balance or name anywhere still gets hidden.

Screenshots

Drag on the slip to draw a box. Tap a box to select it, drag it to move it.

Ready to post

Save opens your phone's share sheet, so you can send a slip straight to Discord or save it to Photos. You can also press and hold an image below.

Tip: in Keep mode, box the time placed too if the slip shows one, so the timestamp stays visible.
